## About

Tyler Dunnington is a singer-songwriter from San Diego whose music draws on his roots in worship and contemporary music. Inspired by artists including Brandon Lake, Switchfoot, and Zach Bryan, Tyler released his debut single in 2025: a cover of "Good Plans" by Red Rocks Worship and Cody Carnes. The song reflects his goal of making music that encourages listeners and points them toward faith.

### Good Living Productions

Good Living Productions produced and released Tyler's debut single, "Good Plans."
